Saved sessions

Stored only on this phone (in this browser). Export to keep a copy: CSV = every beat, same columns as the laptop tool; JSON = summary.

    Vita feed

    While a session is recording, the app sends one reading every 2 minutes (heart rate, RMSSD, SDNN, score, zone, beat counts) to a private GitHub repository, plus a summary when you stop. Raw beat data never leaves the phone. With no token the app makes no network calls at all.

    The token is stored only on this phone (in this browser) and is never sent anywhere except GitHub. Make it a fine-grained token limited to the hrv-sessions repository with Contents: Read and write, expiring in 90 days. You can revoke it on GitHub at any time.

    Keep Bluefy open with the screen on while recording: iOS pauses web pages in the background. Readings that can't be sent (no signal) are kept and sent in order when the connection is back.

    How to use

    1. Open this page in Bluefy (Safari on iPhone has no Bluetooth for web pages).
    2. Put the strap on with wet electrodes. Close Elite HRV and the Polar apps if they are connected to it.
    3. Tap Connect strap, pick your Polar H10 in the list.
    4. Tap Start session to record. Live numbers update every 2 s. Stop to save.
    5. Sessions → CSV/JSON to share or save a file (e.g. to Files or your own cloud).

    What the numbers mean

    • RMSSD: beat-to-beat variation over the last 60 s; higher usually means more relaxed (parasympathetic).
    • Score: ln(RMSSD) scaled to 0–100. Bands are generic; your own trend over weeks is more meaningful than one reading.
    • Walking or moving lowers HRV a lot. For a readiness reading, sit or stand still for 2–3 min.
    • Beats outside 300–2000 ms or >20 % off their neighbours are treated as artefacts (red dots) and left out.

    No accounts, no analytics. All data stays on this phone until you export it, unless you turn on the Vita feed in Settings (then 2-minute readings and session summaries go to your private GitHub repo only).
